# Cortex Agents - Logo Design & Brand Identity Guide

Yeh document **Cortex Agents** ke official logo ki complete technical details aur design philosophy ko cover karta hai. Iska maqsad brand consistency ko barkaraar rakhna hai.

---

## 1. Brand Concept & Vision
Logo ka design **Human Intelligence** aur **Artificial Intelligence** ke fusion ko represent karta hai. 

* **Cortex:** Neural network mesh insani zehen aur data connections ko darshata hai.
* **Agents:** Futuristic robot face software agents aur automation ki power ko represent karta hai.
* **Focus:** Innovation, Speed, aur Intelligent Solutions (Websites, Chatbots, AI Agents).

---

## 2. Design Elements Breakdown

### A. The Cybernetic Icon
* **Neural Network (Left Side):** Ek polygonal geometric mesh jo dots (nodes) se connected hai. Yeh "Connectivity" aur "Deep Learning" ka symbol hai.
* **Android Profile (Right Side):** Ek sleek aur sharp robot face jo "Precision" aur "Action" ko dikhata hai.
* **Energy Nodes:** Logo mein mukhtalif jagaho par white glowing points hain jo "Activity" aur "Data Flow" ko symbolize karte hain.

### B. Typography
* **Font Style:** Modern Sans-Serif (Bold).
* **Case:** All Uppercase (CORTEXAGENTS).
* **Spacing:** Tight tracking takay ek solid aur tech-heavy look nazar aaye.
* **Treatment:** Text par vertical metallic gradient apply kiya gaya hai.

---

## 3. Official Color Palette (Hex Codes)

| Element | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Background** | Pitch Black | `#000000` | Depth aur contrast ke liye. |
| **Highlights** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Glow effects aur bright areas ke liye. |
| **Primary Silver** | Metallic Silver | `#E6E6E6` | Logo elements ki main body ke liye. |
| **Secondary Gray** | Ash Gray | `#A6A6A6` | Shading aur depth ke liye. |
| **Dark Shadow** | Charcoal Gray | `#4D4D4D` | Gradient ke nichle hisse ke liye. |

---

## 4. Visual Effects & Styles

* **Gradient Flow:** * Top: `#FFFFFF`
    * Mid: `#B3B3B3`
    * Bottom: `#737373`
* **Glow Effect:** Logo ke nodes aur aankh (eye) par soft radial blur use kiya gaya hai jis ka color `#FFFFFF` aur opacity 80-90% hai.
* **Shading:** Layered shadows ka istemal kiya gaya hai takay robotic plates mein depth nazar aaye.

---

## 5. Usage Recommendations

1.  **Backgrounds:** Hamesha dark ya black background par use karein takay metallic gradient aur glow sahi se nazar aaye.
2.  **Clear Space:** Logo ke charo taraf munasib jagah chorrein takay iski visibility mutasir na ho.
3.  **Avoid:** Logo ko stretch na karein aur low-resolution environments mein use karne se guraiz karein.
